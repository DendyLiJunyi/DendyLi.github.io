Hi, I’m Junyi Li, also known as Dendy. I’m a mathematics graduate interested in cryptography, functional programming, and formal methods. I completed my M.Sc. in Mathematics at the National University of Singapore in July 2026, after studying Financial Mathematics at Tianjin University.

I’m excited about making the digital world more private, secure, and trustworthy. My interests center on zero-knowledge proofs and zk-SNARKs, and on how programming languages and formal verification can help us build cryptographic systems with precise guarantees. I’m drawn to the connections between mathematical foundations, machine-checked proofs, and working implementations, including applications in blockchains.

Much of my work involves Lean. During my stay at the University of Edinburgh from September 2025 to February 2026, I developed [LeanHammer](https://github.com/DendyLiJunyi/LeanHammer), a Lean 4 tactic that connects proof search to SMT solvers. It selects relevant lemmas, translates goals for Z3 and cvc5, and supports partial proof reconstruction with Duper. Earlier, as a research intern at Huawei’s Singapore Research Center, I worked on formalizing mathematics problems in Lean and combining language models with automated theorem proving.

I’ve also worked on formalizations in algebra and calculus, and contributed to ArkLib, a Lean library for formally verified arguments of knowledge. Schools in programming languages, proof and computation, and univalent mathematics have given me opportunities to explore the broader ideas behind this work.

This blog is a place for notes on what I’m learning, the projects I’m working on, and reflections along the way.
